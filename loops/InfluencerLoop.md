# Influencer Loop — content → engagement → conversion, under governance

Sibling to `IncomeStreamsLoop.md`. The loop the first AI influencer company runs:

1. **Draft** — persona-authored content inside declared topic bounds
2. **Compliance** — structural checks (disclosure, dark patterns, finance
   hardening, legal escalation); refusal is a record, not an exception
3. **Human gate** — publish-ready package with `requires_human_approval: true`;
   nothing self-executes
4. **Publish (gated)** — the caller routes the package through the kernel
   Consequence Gate; only authorized artifacts leave
5. **Reality** — engagement quality + complaint rate flow back in
6. **Kill criteria** — breach pauses the persona immediately (quality floor,
   complaint ceiling, off-brand budget)
7. **Learn** — rabbit-hole depth analytics sharpen the next sequence; audience
   pains become OpportunityPackets through the intake bridge

The loop deepens trust, never dependence: every rabbit-hole step carries an
exit ramp, and depth is earned by consumption, never manufactured.
